**CONTEXT disagreements, summarized:**
- MRTR.5/.6: `Keyring`, `ConsumedLedger`, `InFlight` DO have non-test constructors, and `AppState` DOES carry a `continuation: Arc<ContinuationState>` field (`src/gateway/router/mod.rs:93`, built at `src/gateway/server/mod.rs:1171`). The prior audit's specific claim ("zero non-test constructors", "no field") is factually wrong. The functional conclusion (MRTR.5/.6 unwired) still holds, but for a different reason: the field is constructed and then never read — no `.keyring()`/`.ledger()`/`.in_flight()` call exists anywhere in `src/` outside test files.
- MRTR.4: the "generic test that cannot fail against a short-circuiting comparison" describes a *different*, honestly-scoped test (`a_wrong_binding_of_any_length_is_refused_identically`, about timing side-channels, not binding correctness). The two dedicated `ac_mrtr_4_*` tests are real, falsifiable checks of the actual MRTR.4 requirement. Both `redeemable_by` itself and everything upstream of it remain unwired in production, so MRTR.4 is still correctly UNWIRED overall — just not for the reason stated.
- MRTR.7: CONTEXT's claim on `Bridge::to_legacy_client` having no caller is confirmed exactly as stated.
- MRTR.1/.2/.3/.9: CONTEXT's core claims (handlers.rs:873-889 blanket refusal; MRTR.9 has no mechanism) are confirmed as stated.
