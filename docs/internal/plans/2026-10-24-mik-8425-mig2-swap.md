### 3a. G3 reachability, by type, before P3's rows are recorded

The G3 set is the census's **externally tagged enums with data variants** (9, table row 2). It is not the 9 internally tagged ones (row 4) and not the 7 untagged ones (row 3): those keep accepting maps and are P3's positive controls, never refuse rows.

The census's file heuristic is not evidence. Both r1 seats showed it was wrong: `CompiledBundle` is a false positive (`runtime/provision.rs:254` reads `SandboxDescriptor` and builds the bundle afterwards), and `RuntimeNetworkEgress` is reached through `Config.runtime.profiles`, not `commands/trust/lab.rs`. Step 2 therefore walks the field graph from every adapter read target (each `yaml::from_str::<T>`, `from_value::<T>` and `documents` consumer, plus figment's `Config` extract) to each of the 9 enums, and records each path or its absence in the census. P3 pins exactly the reachable set, and every reachable enum gets a tag-form accept row and a map-form refuse row.
