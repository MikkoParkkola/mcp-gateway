- **Provenance receipts can carry a publicly verifiable Ed25519 signature.**
  When `GATEWAY_PROVENANCE_SIGNING_SEED` and `GATEWAY_PROVENANCE_KEY_ID` are set, stamping adds `public_signature` beside the HMAC, exported with `mcp-gateway provenance public-key` and checked with `mcp-gateway provenance verify` without any gateway secret. Step receipts in chain and playbook answers carry the public signature. Unset, output is byte-identical. See UPGRADING-4.0. (#8398)
  Affects: 4.0.0 pre-releases only; 3.x unaffected. Operator action: optional; to enable, set the seed (base64 of 32 random bytes) and key id, restart, and publish the JWK.
