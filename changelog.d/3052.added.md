- **`schedule.tick` event source.** With `events.sources.schedule: true`, a client can subscribe to a wake-up on a five-field cron expression read in an IANA timezone (UTC by default). A skipped or repeated daylight-saving hour fires once. At most one tick every 5 minutes; an optional label of up to 64 characters comes back in each tick. Ticks missed while the gateway is down are not sent late, and a restart within the same minute does not repeat one. Bounds: `events.schedule.max_timers` (default 1000) and `max_timers_per_principal` (default 20). Off by default. See `docs/EVENTS.md`. (#3052, MIK-7720)
