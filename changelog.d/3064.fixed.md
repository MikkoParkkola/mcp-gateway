- **A reserved name in a CLI or MCP capability's `env` list no longer blanks its value in results.**
  `HOME` and the other names the gateway sets for the child itself never reach it, yet their
  operator values were still scrubbed from tool results as if they were injected secrets. They
  are now left out of the redaction list, as they are out of the child's environment; declared
  names the child receives are still scrubbed. (#3064)
  Affects: 4.0.0 pre-releases only; 3.x unaffected. Operator action: none.
