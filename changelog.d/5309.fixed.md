- **A gateway stop now ends capability MCP servers gracefully.** Over HTTP and stdio, the per-caller servers an MCP capability starts are handed to the stop with the rest of the gateway's backends: their stdin is closed first, so they can save before any signal, and the stop waits for them within its deadline. Before, they ran on until the process exited and were then killed without notice. A call still in flight on such a server ends with an error at once.
