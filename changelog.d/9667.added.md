- `mcp-gateway api` now streams the two UI API routes that answer a stream or a download instead of reading them whole. `GET /ui/api/stream` prints events as they arrive and stops after `--limit N` events; `--limit` is required there and refused on every other route. `GET /ui/api/activity/export` prints each line as it arrives. Every printed line or event passes the credential redactor first, and one the redactor cannot mask stops the run without being printed. A streamed answer is bounded: 30 s to the response headers, 30 s with no bytes, 30 s for the next complete event, and 5 minutes overall for the stream or 10 minutes for the export, writes to stdout included, so a stalled reader cannot hold the command open. A single line or event over 16 MiB is refused unprinted. `--data` and `--if-match` are refused on the two streamed routes. Any other answer on those routes (an error status or another content type) is read whole as before, within the usual 30 s.
