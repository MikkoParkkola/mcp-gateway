- **A question the gateway withheld no longer holds a continuation slot until it expires.** When a backend asked the client a question over `POST /mcp` (without an event stream) or `POST /mcp/{name}`, and the gateway then withheld the answer (a cross-tenant read blocked, a delivery or invocation record that could not be written, a failed grant-decision write, or a withheld confirmation challenge), the question's slot in the 4096-slot continuation table stayed taken until it expired. These slots are now given back as soon as the answer is withheld, so a burst of refusals can no longer exhaust the table. Delivered questions keep their slot for the client's retry. Affects 4.0.0 pre-releases only (MIK-8176).
