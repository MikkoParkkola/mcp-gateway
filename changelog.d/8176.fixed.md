- **A question the gateway withheld no longer holds a continuation slot until it expires.** When a backend asked the client a question and the gateway then withheld the answer (a cross-tenant read blocked, a delivery or invocation record that could not be written, a failed grant-decision write, or a withheld confirmation challenge), the question's slot in the 4096-slot continuation table stayed taken until it expired. These slots are now given back as soon as the answer is withheld, on `POST /mcp` (with or without an event stream), `POST /mcp/{name}` and stdio, so a burst of refusals can no longer exhaust the table. On stdio, an answer still waiting to be written when the session ends also gives its slot back. Delivered questions keep their slot for the client's retry. Affects 4.0.0 pre-releases only (MIK-8176).
