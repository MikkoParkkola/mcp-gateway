- The cost-governance budget enforcer no longer keeps a day accumulator for every name that ever spent. Entries from earlier days without a budget are swept, and past 256 unbudgeted names per map (or a name over 256 bytes), spend goes to a separate overflow total. Before this, with a backend's input-schema check off and a non-zero `default_cost`, every tool name a caller invented added a permanent entry (MIK-8015).
  Affects: the 4.0.0 pre-releases and 3.x. Operator action: none. Budget checks are unchanged; the admin stats and saved costs gain `tool_overflow_spend_usd`/`key_overflow_spend_usd` (stats) and `tool_overflow_usd`/`key_overflow_usd` (saved file, defaulting to 0 for older files).
