- **Seven settings the gateway can pick itself are retired before 4.0.0 GA.** They are `capabilities.stale_check_secs` (the check runs every 60 s), `cache.enabled` (the cache is always on, for read-only annotated tools only), `failsafe.retry.enabled` (retry follows the resend annotations), `failsafe.circuit_breaker.enabled` (the breaker is always on), the nested `auth.client_circuit_breaker.enabled` (the section's presence is the switch), `security.signature_chain.max_links` (a constant 8) and `server.protocol_revision_window` (now the hidden `--protocol-revision-window` flag or `MCP_GATEWAY_PROTOCOL_REVISION_WINDOW`). A config that still sets one loads, logs one warning naming the key and what to do, and does not have the value honoured. `mcp-gateway upgrade` deletes each one. It keeps a lone `client_circuit_breaker.enabled` and says to write `client_circuit_breaker: {}`. (MIK-8782, MIK-8783)
