- Stdio backends receive six operator-level npm settings from the gateway's environment: `npm_config_allow_git`, `cafile`, `loglevel`, `offline`, `prefer_offline` and `strict_ssl`, matched in any spelling. It is an allowlist, so credentials and the per-backend cache stay behind, and a setting a backend names in its own `env:` wins. Forwarding `strict_ssl`, `cafile` or `allow_git` sets that policy for every backend that leaves it unset ([@terafin](https://github.com/terafin), [#1760](https://github.com/MikkoParkkola/mcp-gateway/pull/1760)).
