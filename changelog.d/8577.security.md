- **TLS no longer trusts the distrusted emSign intermediates.** The certificate verifier every rustls connection uses (`rustls-webpki` 0.103.16, RUSTSEC-2026-0382) is updated, and so are the two bundled Mozilla root sets: `webpki-roots` 1.0.10 (RUSTSEC-2026-0381), which WebSocket backends trust, and `webpki-root-certs` 1.0.10 (RUSTSEC-2026-0383), which the platform verifier pulls in. A server certificate chained through one of those intermediates is refused. (#8577)
  Affects: 3.5.0, 3.5.1 and the 4.0.0 pre-releases. Operator action: none for a backend with a publicly trusted certificate; a WebSocket or HTTPS backend whose certificate chains through an emSign intermediate now fails its TLS handshake and needs a certificate from another authority.
