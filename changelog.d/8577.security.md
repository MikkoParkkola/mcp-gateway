- **TLS no longer trusts the distrusted emSign intermediates.** The rustls certificate verifier `rustls-webpki` is updated to 0.103.16 (RUSTSEC-2026-0382), and so are the two bundled Mozilla root sets: `webpki-roots` 1.0.10 (RUSTSEC-2026-0381), which WebSocket backends trust, and `webpki-root-certs` 1.0.10 (RUSTSEC-2026-0383), which the platform verifier pulls in. A server certificate chained through one of those intermediates is refused wherever these crates do the check; on macOS and Windows, HTTPS backends are checked by the operating system's trust store, which these crates do not change. (#8577)
  Affects: 2.0.0 up to 3.5.1 and the 4.0.0 pre-releases. Operator action: none for a backend with a publicly trusted certificate; a WebSocket or HTTPS backend whose certificate chains through an emSign intermediate now fails its TLS handshake and needs a certificate from another authority.
