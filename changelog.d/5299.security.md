- **With an audit log configured, the gateway now writes (or attempts to write) an audit record for every refused call on `/mcp` and stdio, with its own decision kind.** A call refused before it was admitted (a `gateway_invoke` missing its server or tool, a sessionless profile change, a key reused across protocol revisions, and the like) was answered with no record; it is now recorded first, with the decision kind its check noted, else `unrecorded`. A stdio call with malformed retry fields is recorded the way `/mcp` records it. A keyed plan's hidden or absent step no longer lends its decision kind to a different refusal of the same request. Answers do not change, except that one of these refusals that a fail-closed audit log cannot record now answers that the log is unavailable (HTTP 503, `-32005`); under a best-effort log the refusal is answered as before.
  Affects: 4.0.0 pre-release builds. Operator action: none.
