- **`keychain:NAME` secret references.** Every gateway.yaml secret slot that accepts `env:` and `file:` now accepts `keychain:NAME`, naming an OS keychain entry (ADR-028): `auth.bearer_token`, `auth.api_keys[*].key_sha256`, `agent_auth.agents[*].hs256_secret`, `server.metrics_token`, `security.message_signing.shared_secret` and `previous_secret`, the transparency-log shared secret, `security.signature_chain.signing_key`, the record principal key, `key_server.admin_token`, `accounts.keys`, adapter `hmac_secret_ref` and `client_secret_ref`. The entry's bytes are used exactly, with nothing trimmed. A name outside `[A-Za-z0-9._-]{1,128}` fails the load naming the field only, since that text may be a mistyped secret. A missing, empty, locked or non-UTF-8 entry fails naming the field and the entry. The value is never named. (MIK-8908)
