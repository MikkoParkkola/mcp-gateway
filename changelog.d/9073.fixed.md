- **A stderr nobody reads no longer stalls requests.** Log lines go through a bounded queue to one writer thread; when stderr stops draining, lines past the queue are dropped and counted, and the gateway reports the count in a line of its own once stderr takes writes again. No log line is written while a lock is held any more, so a slow log can no longer hold up other requests through a shared lock. Audit records with their own file are unaffected.
