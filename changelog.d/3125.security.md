- With authentication off, a legacy client that does not resume an established session no longer gets a new identity for the per-caller controls on every request. The tenant guard, anomaly detector and call budget now count all such requests under one shared identity, so tenant breadth, anomaly history and the call budget accumulate across them instead of restarting each time (MIK-7971).
  Affects: the 4.0.0 pre-releases. Operator action: with authentication off and the tenant guard, call budget or anomaly blocking enabled, session-less legacy clients now share one limit; raise the limit or have clients reuse their session if they are refused.
