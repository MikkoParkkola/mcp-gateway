- A legacy client that calls without a credential (authentication off, or on with the path public, as `/mcp` is in the shipped presets) and does not resume an established session no longer gets a new identity for the per-caller controls on every request. The tenant guard, anomaly detector and call budget now count all such requests under one shared identity, so tenant breadth, anomaly history and the call budget accumulate across them instead of restarting each time (MIK-7971).
  Affects: the 4.0.0 pre-releases. Operator action: with the tenant guard, call budget or anomaly blocking enabled, keyless session-less legacy clients now share one limit; give them a credential, raise the limit, or have them reuse their session if they are refused.
