- **`tools/list_changed` reaches a caller only when that caller's own list changed.** Before, a change to a tool a caller could not see (a policy-denied tool, another principal's per-user slot, a backend outside its key's scope) still sent every connected stream a `tools/list_changed`, which told a probing client that something hidden had moved. Now the legacy GET stream, `subscriptions/listen` and stdio each judge the hint against the view the stream's own caller is shown, under the identities the stream was opened with (key, OAuth agent, certificate, Open WebUI end user, Cloudflare Access assertion, trusted-proxy peer). A hint that is owed is written once; a repeat of a view the caller already holds is not written. (MIK-8350)
  Affects: clients that refetch `tools/list` on every `tools/list_changed`; they now refetch less often.
  Operator action: none.
- **A stream whose caller no longer resolves when it opens is refused like an invalid credential, and one whose identity lapses while open is closed.** A GET or `subscriptions/listen` opened by a caller found dead at attach answers 401 `Invalid token`, byte for byte as an invalid credential does, with the reason only in the `mcp_view_liveness_dead_total{reason}` counter. An open stream is closed when its Open WebUI assertion expires or its held Cloudflare Access assertion stops verifying. A caller that presented no credential on a public path still opens its stream and is told nothing. (MIK-8350)
  Affects: clients that held a stream past the lifetime of the identity it was opened under.
  Operator action: none; clients reconnect with a current credential.
