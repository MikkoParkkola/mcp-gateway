- **A stdio request no longer copies its whole dispatch to the heap a second time.** Since MIK-8154's intake change, every stdio request boxed its dispatch inside the per-request visibility record, although the serve loop already boxes that dispatch. That added about 5 KB per request, a refused one included: a refused signed `gateway_invoke` over stdio allocated about 14 KB instead of about 9 KB. It now boxes once again. (MIK-8400)
  Affects: stdio deployments, mostly under a stream of refused calls.
  Operator action: none.
