- **Where each API key stands against its own daily limit.** Each `by_key` row of `GET /ui/api/costs` now carries `at_limit` (what reaching the limit does: `"block"`, `"notify"` or `null`), `blocking` (the cheapest priced call made with the key is refused now), `blocked_since` (unix seconds of today's first such refusal, `null` after a restart until the next one), `rate_3h_usd_per_hour` (spend over the last three complete hours, divided by three), `today_usd` (today's spend as the budget check judges it) and `daily_limit_usd`. The body gains `resets_at`, the next local midnight, and `alert_rules: "unordered"` when a milder rule sits above a stricter one. Additive only; `/api/costs` is unchanged.
