- **OAuth: a renewal or login that spans an issuer switch hands over nothing of the old issuer.** A background renewal whose client-credentials answer arrives after the backend was signed in again under another issuer no longer reports or logs "Token renewed via client_credentials": it is deferred, as a renewal that starts after the switch already was. `OAuthClient::authorize()` now refuses (`AuthorizationRequired`) when such a switch lands during the login, instead of returning the old issuer's token; the gateway's own callers already refused it. Library callers are unaffected: a switch exists only for a client given the gateway's sign-in status, which code outside the crate cannot attach. Nothing of the old issuer was ever cached. (MIK-9165)
