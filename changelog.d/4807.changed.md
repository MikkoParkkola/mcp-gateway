- The key server's per-identity token cap holds under concurrent mints: a mint reserves its slot before the token exists, and the token is valid only once the mint finishes. Revoking an identity mid-mint cancels the mint (403); a mint that outlives its 30-second reservation answers 503 with Retry-After 1. (MIK-9386)
- Breaking (library): `TokenStore::insert` is removed in favour of `reserve`, `activate` and `abort`; `InMemoryTokenStore::new` takes the cap and token lifetime and the type no longer implements `Default`. See UPGRADING.
