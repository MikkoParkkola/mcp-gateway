- **A daily anonymous heartbeat.** A released server (`serve` or the stdio server) sends at most one POST per UTC day per data directory to `https://telemetry.revaluator.ai/v1/heartbeat`, with five fields: `project`, `event`, the version, the OS and architecture, and a random `install_id` kept in `<data dir>/heartbeat/`. It never sends from a development build, CI, `security.posture: hardened`, or under `HTTPS_PROXY`, `HTTP_PROXY` or `ALL_PROXY`, and `DO_NOT_TRACK=1` (or `NO_TELEMETRY=1`) turns it off. A failure is silent and nothing waits for it. docs/PRIVACY.md, "Daily heartbeat", states what is sent, what the receiver stores and for how long.
