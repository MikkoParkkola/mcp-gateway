- **Event subscriptions follow backend and catalogue changes without waiting for a sweep.** Removing a backend now withdraws all of its event subscriptions, not only `tools_changed`. A backend that becomes ineligible loses its upstream event subscriptions. A re-added backend is listened to at once. Expired and held subscriptions stop their upstream work at the next worker tick. A subscription re-made while an older withdrawal or revocation was in flight is no longer deleted by it. Affects 4.0.0 pre-releases only (MIK-8179, MIK-8180, MIK-8178, MIK-7897).
