- **A direct call's record says what its tool declares.** A `tools/call` on `/mcp/{backend}` now records `declared_effect` and `declared_effect_source` on its decision, read from the slot the call runs under: the caller's own per-user slot when its identity is propagated, otherwise the shared one. A repeated keyed call answered from the idempotency cache records the caller's slot as it is when the answer is served; one answered from a completed execution records the slot that execution ran on, looked up as stored, never the replaying caller's or the shared slot's. A slot that cannot say records no effect. A direct call naming a capability provider is answered as absent and records no grant. (MIK-9216)
