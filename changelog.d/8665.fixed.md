- **Warm-start retry gaps under a millisecond are no longer always zero.** Warm-start spread each retry gap over `[0, gap]` in whole milliseconds, so any gap below 1 ms drew 0 every time and the retry ran with no wait. Warm-start and chain-step retries now share one jitter helper that draws in nanoseconds. The shipped 2 s, 30 s and 60 s gaps keep the same spread. (MIK-8665)
