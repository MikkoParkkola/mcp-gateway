- **Cost-governance budgets and alert rules apply on reload.** A per-tool, per-key or daily budget, or an alert rule, changed by a config reload judges the next call. A request in flight keeps the budgets it started with, a queued task judges the budgets current when it dispatches, and today's spend and in-flight reservations carry over. The restart report no longer lists `cost_governance` for a budget- or alert-only edit. Turning governance on or off, `tool_costs`, `default_cost` and `alternatives` still need a restart. (MIK-8861)
