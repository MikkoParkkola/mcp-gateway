- **`api` shows the revision a capability save needs, and how to recover without it.** A successful answer that carries an `ETag` prints the revision on stderr, ready to pass with `--if-match`. A capability save refused for a missing revision (428) or a stale one (409) now says to load it again with `api GET`, apply the change to the text that load returns, and send the revision it printed, so a change someone else made in the meantime is kept. A refused delete says to load it, confirm that is the version to remove, and delete with the revision that load printed. Other 428 answers keep their generic remedy.
  Affects: scripts that match the old remedy text of a capability 428 or 409, or that expect nothing on stderr after a successful answer carrying an `ETag`. Operator action: none.
