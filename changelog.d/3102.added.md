- MCP events: gateway operational events, off by default (`events.sources.operational`): `gateway.budget.threshold` (50, 80 and 100 % of a daily budget), `gateway.budget.exhausted`, `gateway.backend.health_changed` (a backend's shared-slot circuit breaker) and `gateway.kill_switch.changed`. Health and kill-switch events reach admins only; a non-admin API-key holder receives only its own key's budget events, and callers without an API key have no budget of their own. Budget deliveries are not charged. (#3102, MIK-7720)
