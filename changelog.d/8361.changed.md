- **A keyed chain with a step missing on a per-user backend now runs its earlier steps, as it does on a shared backend.** A keyed `gateway_execute` chain (one that carries an idempotency key) whose step names a tool missing from the caller's own catalogue used to be refused whole before any step ran. Now each step is judged when the chain reaches it, so earlier steps run and the chain stops at that step with the missing-name answer. Per-user and shared backends now answer alike, and a step the caller may not see answers exactly as a missing one. (MIK-8361)
  Affects: clients that send keyed chains to per-user backends.
  Operator action: none.
