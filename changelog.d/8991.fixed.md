- **A call refused by the OAuth destination check records `response_effect: refused`.** Three token paths raise that refusal in a task of their own, out of reach of the call's delivery record: a tool call's token refresh, a backend's OAuth handshake when the call starts it, and a capability's token refresh. Each record said `delivered` beside the refusal the caller received. The refusal is now recorded where the call picks it up. Health probes and upstream task queries still record nothing, because they turn the error into an answer of their own. (MIK-8991)
