- **A capability or OAuth-destination refusal of a call records `response_effect: refused`.** These refusals reached the caller as an error while the call's delivery record said `delivered`: a capability refused inside a read-only call, a CLI or MCP capability naming a network destination, a capability URL that is private, names loopback or sends a credential in cleartext (REST, GraphQL, JSON-RPC and the token endpoint), and a send the address pin refuses. Three OAuth token paths raise their refusal in a task of their own, out of reach of the call's record: a tool call's token refresh, a backend's OAuth handshake when the call starts it, and a capability's token refresh; each is now recorded where the call picks it up. Answers are unchanged. Health probes and upstream task queries still record nothing, because they turn the error into an answer of their own. (MIK-8991)
