- **The governance audit reads past a large record.** Reading the control-plane audit log refused any record longer than its 1 MiB read window, though the log accepts records up to 4 MiB, so one large policy change or decision made every older page an error. Such records now read back whole. A line over the 4 MiB cap, which the log never writes, is reported once and skipped: the page names it in the new `AuditPage::unreadable` field (its offset and segment), the older pages still read, the admin view marks itself degraded, and grant-change recovery refuses rather than guess past it. A page now reads at most 1 MiB plus one 4 MiB record. Both the governance reader and Activity now refuse a page whose log segment was removed by retention while it was being opened; Activity used to read the active file in its place, which holds a different segment. (MIK-8931)
