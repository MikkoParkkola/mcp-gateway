- **Three more refusals of a signed call give its nonce back when nothing it did had acted.**
  The idempotency guard's own refusal (a full key cache, a key reused for another request, a key already in
  flight), the undeclared-argument refusal, and a chain step's dispatch-time policy refusal kept the signing
  nonce spent, so the honest call re-sent under that nonce was refused as a replay. They now follow the same
  rule as the relay refusal: the nonce stays spent if a credential was minted, a tool list was fetched from
  the backend or an earlier chain step was sent first. (MIK-8353)
  Affects: 4.0.0 pre-releases only. Operator action: none.
