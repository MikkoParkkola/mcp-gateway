- **Token refreshes give up on a busy credential lock instead of waiting forever (MIK-8619).**
  An interactive token refresh waits at most 90 seconds for another gateway process's credential
  lock. Background and non-interactive work, such as a health check, waits at most 2 seconds.
  Background renewal keeps the 90-second wait, then tries again at its next period. A busy lock
  answers "try again later", with no login prompt and no transport rebuild. A caller whose own
  timeout is shorter than the lock wait, such as a health check or a backend call, can still time
  out as before (MIK-8624).
