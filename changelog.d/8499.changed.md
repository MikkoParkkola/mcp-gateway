- The input sanitizer (`security.sanitize_input`, on by default) no longer rebuilds a clean request: it now edits the request in place and copies only what it rewrites. Sanitizing a clean 1 or 3 MiB `tools/call` allocates a fixed amount (it allocated about 9 MB and 27 MB), so a refused 1 MiB call no longer costs about 9 MB more with the sanitizer on. What it refuses and rewrites is unchanged (MIK-8499).
