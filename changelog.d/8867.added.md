- **Gateway restart refusal.** The bundled UI's admin-only `GET /ui/api/gateway/restart` reports `available=false` with platform-specific recovery guidance; `POST` accepts a strict `{confirm: string}` JSON body and records an attributed audit entry for its 409 refusal. The call is refused and the process does not restart: 4.0.0 has no in-process restart. While a reload or settings save leaves restart-only settings pending, its answer says how to restart the gateway yourself.
