- **Each request's dispatch future is no longer held twice.** The wrapper that opens a request's delivery record was an `async fn` around the whole dispatch future, so the boxed future kept a second copy of it: 23,736 B per request, half of it the copy. It and 13 other wrappers of the same shape now return the scoped future directly, so a request, refused or answered, allocates about 11.8 KB less. Behaviour is unchanged. (MIK-9396)
