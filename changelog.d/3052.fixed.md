- Scheduler: a weekday step `*/n` now matches only the days `n` divides; each day was also tested as its number plus 7 (meant only as Sunday's alias), so `*/2` matched every day and `*/3` to `*/13` (except `*/7`) matched extra days. Scheduled jobs and `schedule.tick` subscriptions with such a step fire on fewer days after upgrading; `*/1`, `*/7` and `*/14` or more are unchanged. (#3052, MIK-7720)
  Affects: 3.x and 4.0.0 pre-releases. Operator action: check any scheduled job whose weekday field uses a step.
