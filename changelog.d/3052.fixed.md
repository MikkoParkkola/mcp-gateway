- Scheduler: a stepped weekday field such as `*/2` now matches only its own days; it used to match every day, because 7 was tested as an alias for each weekday instead of only for Sunday. Scheduled jobs and `schedule.tick` subscriptions with a stepped weekday field fire on fewer days after upgrading. (#3052, MIK-7720)
  Affects: 3.x and 4.0.0 pre-releases. Operator action: check any scheduled job whose weekday field uses a step.
