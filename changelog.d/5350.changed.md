- **Local command errors have one shape.** When `init`, the `tls` commands, `doctor`, `identity grants`, `import preview` and `apply`, `ranking eval`, `setup wizard` and `export`, `stats`, `upgrade` or `cap import-url` fail, stderr holds two lines: `error: <what happened>`, then what to do. They exit 1, or 2 when the command was called wrong, and `--json` leaves the error as these two human lines. Commands not converted yet keep their current output.
- **Two wrong invocations now exit 2 instead of 1:** an unknown `doctor --shadow-format`, and a `tls issue-server` or `tls issue-client` CA key path that is not valid UTF-8.
- **`import preview` and `import apply` with `--format json` exit 1 when the result cannot be written as JSON.** They printed the error and exited 0.
- **`setup export` and `upgrade` say why they exit 1.** `setup export` with client configs it could not write, and `upgrade` without `--quiet` when keys it cannot remove block the config, now end with the two error lines; before, they exited 1 with nothing on stderr. See `docs/UPGRADING-4.0.md`.
