- **An mTLS refusal's decision record names the policy rule that made it.** A refusal decided by a configured mTLS rule records a stable `rule_id`: `mtls:` and an HMAC of the configured rule under the same key the firewall and tool-policy ids use. The same rule keeps its id across restarts, and an edited rule gets a new one. A refusal with no client certificate, or with no matching rule, records kind `mtls` without a rule id. A disabled policy records nothing. On the direct route, a policy that refuses every tool on a backend still answers "not found", as before. Identity grants also get keyed ids, kept beside the grant rows so the grant digest is unchanged. A grant refusal records no id, because no grant decides a refusal. (MIK-9199)
