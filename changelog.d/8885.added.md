- The new web UI has one tooltip for every setting, number and state: an (i) button next to the subject, named "About …", whose text a screen reader reads once as the button's description. It opens on hover after 300 ms, at once on keyboard focus, Enter or Space, and on tap; it closes on Esc, blur, tapping outside, or 150 ms after the pointer leaves, and stays open while the pointer is over it. It stays on screen and never covers its subject. Tooltip text ships as one cached file the screens read by name; if it fails to load, pages render without tooltips. The accessibility check now runs on every page and tab at phone and desktop widths, in dark and light, and with a tooltip open. (MIK-8885)
