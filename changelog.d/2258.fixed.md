- **Backend names that differ only in punctuation or case no longer share a package cache, and bunx and yarn get their own.** `team.alpha` and `team_alpha` mapped to one cache directory, and `bunx` and `yarn` were given `npm_config_cache`, which they do not read. The cache directory name now carries a hash of the full name, and `bunx` gets `BUN_INSTALL_CACHE_DIR` and `yarn` gets `YARN_CACHE_FOLDER`. pnpm keeps its shared store and is no longer given a variable it ignores. Package caches are downloaded once more after the upgrade. (#2258)
