- **A wake from sleep no longer stops every backend for hours.** The per-backend rate limiter read a raw hardware counter, and a bucket whose stored state was ahead of that clock refused every call until the clock caught up: after one wake from hibernate on an Apple Silicon laptop, every backend answered "Rate limit exceeded" for hours, until a restart. The limiter now reads `std::time::Instant`, and a refusal that asks for a longer wait than one refill interval, which only a clock that went back produces, rebuilds the bucket and admits the call. Each reset logs a WARN `backend rate limiter clock stepped back; bucket reset` with the backend and the step, and a refusing backend logs `backend rate limiter refused` with its next allowed time, at most once a minute per backend. (MIK-9308)
