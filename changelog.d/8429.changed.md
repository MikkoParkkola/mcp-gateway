- A playbook step whose tool result has `isError: true` is now a failed step that follows `on_error` (abort, continue, retry) instead of a completed one. A step that meets a busy managed-account custody ends the playbook instead of being null-filled: with the retryable error when nothing in the playbook was tried before it, and with a non-retryable error otherwise. Under `on_error: retry` an unkeyed playbook sends a failed step again. See UPGRADING (MIK-8429).
