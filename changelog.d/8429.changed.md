- A playbook step whose tool result has `isError: true` is now a failed step that follows `on_error` (abort, continue, retry) instead of a completed one. A step that meets a busy managed-account custody ends the playbook instead of being null-filled: with the retryable error when nothing in the playbook was tried before it, and with a non-retryable error otherwise. `on_error: retry` no longer retries tool-reported errors (a call that failed in the gateway is still re-sent; keyed calls replay its stored failure), and a tool's text is kept out of the error message (at most 256 characters plus a cut marker, in the error's data). See UPGRADING (MIK-8429).
