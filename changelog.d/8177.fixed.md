- **A refused state-only round no longer holds a continuation slot until it expires.** An `input_required` round that carries only state and asks the client nothing kept its continuation slot until expiry when the gateway refused it (the firewall, or a delivery record that could not be written), on `POST /mcp`, stdio and tasks (the initial round and a resumed one). The slot is now given back when the round is refused. Affects 4.0.0 pre-releases only (MIK-8177).
