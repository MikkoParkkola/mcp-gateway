- **`secrets.store` setting.** Secrets saved through the gateway go to a fresh entry in the chosen store, all or nothing, and an old entry is never deleted. `files` writes a new owner-only file under `<config dir>/secrets/` and references it with `file:`. `auto`, the default, uses `files` until the keychain writer lands, and `keychain` is refused until then by name. (MIK-8908)
