- **API keys rotate without a gap.** An `auth.api_keys[]` entry accepts three optional rotation fields (ADR-022): `previous_key_sha256`, a previous digest accepted until `previous_expires_at`, and `principal`, which keeps the key's identity, and so its sessions, grants, journals and tasks, across a new digest. Both digests are matched in constant time, and a key with no previous digest can never match one. Load refuses a previous digest without an expiry, an explicit `principal` that another credential already resolves to, and any two equal digests among every key's current and previous digest and the bearer token. A previous digest takes the same `env:`, `file:` and `keychain:` references as `key_sha256`, and an adapter secret may not reuse it. A key without the fields behaves exactly as before. (MIK-8862)
