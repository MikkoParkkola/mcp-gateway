- **Tool ranking reads a risk label the same way for safety and risk.** A catalogue
  entry's `risk_level` (or `risk`) label was read two ways: the risk signal ignored
  case and treated `blocked` as high risk, but the safety signal matched only
  lower-case `high`, `critical` and `unsafe`. A tool labelled `HIGH`, `Critical`,
  `UNSAFE` or `blocked` scored zero on risk but full marks on safety. It was already
  left out of results through its risk score. Its safety score is now zero as well,
  and the ranking explanation gives its exclusion reason as `suppressed_unsafe`
  instead of `suppressed_high_risk`. (MIK-8549)
  Affects: ranking explanations for tools whose risk label is not lower case, or is `blocked`.
  Operator action: none; a script matching the old exclusion reason for such tools should match `suppressed_unsafe` too.
