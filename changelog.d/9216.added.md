- **A replayed call's record says what its tool declares.** A completed call served again from its stored result (the HTTP and stdio meta routes, and a per-caller replay lifted after its live proof) now records `declared_effect` and `declared_effect_source` on its `replayed` decision, as a live call does. The effect is read when the replay is served, from the slot the first execution ran under, so an edited declaration shows on the next replay; a capability's comes from its live definition, as the replaying caller sees it. A backend tool's replay records no effect when its result ran under more than one slot, was stored before this change, or its slot, tool or backend is gone, never another slot's; a capability's needs no stored slot, and records none once its definition is removed or hidden from the caller. A continuation records none. (MIK-9216)
