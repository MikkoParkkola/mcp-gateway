- **A replayed call's record says what its tool declares.** A completed call served again from its stored result (the HTTP and stdio meta routes, and a per-caller replay lifted after its live proof) now records `declared_effect` and `declared_effect_source` on its `replayed` decision, as a live call does. The effect is read when the replay is served, from the slot the first execution ran under, so an edited declaration shows on the next replay; a capability's comes from its live definition, as the replaying caller sees it. A result whose steps ran under more than one slot, one stored before this change, a continuation, or a slot, tool, backend or definition that is gone records no effect, never another slot's. (MIK-9216)
