- **Event subscription handles and new `eventId`s are keyed aliases; the admin dead-letter listing names `subscriptionAlias`.** An `events/subscribe` answer, the `x-mcp-subscription-id` header and a new event's `eventId` (and `webhook-id`) change once at the upgrade, and again if the record key changes; `subscriptionId` leaves the dead-letter listing, whose `subscription` filter still takes a stored id.
