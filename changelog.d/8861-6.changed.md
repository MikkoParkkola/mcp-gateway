- **A budget added by reload starts from the spend it may already have.** Past the 256 per-name rows a day keeps for unbudgeted tools and keys, later spend is kept only as one overflow total. A per-tool or per-key budget a reload adds for a name with no row of its own now starts from that overflow total (or from the overflow total when the name's row was created that day), an upper bound of its earlier spend, instead of from zero. The figure is taken once a day, kept apart from real spend in `costs.json` (`tool_estimate`, `key_estimate`) and through a restart, and `gateway_get_stats` reports it per budgeted name as `tool_estimated_start_usd` / `key_estimated_start_usd` (null when none was taken). (MIK-8861)
