- **Operational events accept their subscribers.** The budget, backend-health and kill-switch
  events could be listed but not subscribed to over `/mcp`: every bearer and API-key caller got
  `-32012`, because the gateway judged the caller's owner key against a differently spelled form of
  the same credential. One form is used for both now, so an admin key and the static bearer can
  subscribe to all four types and a key holder to its own key's budget events. A caller that is not
  a configured credential (an mTLS subject, an OIDC or key-server identity) still cannot. (#5268)
