- **A stdio answer over the size limit fails only its own call, and is not retried.** When a
  stdio backend answered with a message over its `max_frame_bytes`, every call waiting on that
  backend failed with "Response channel closed" marked retryable, so clients repeated a call that
  fails the same way and each retry restarted the backend again. Now the call whose answer it was
  gets `RESPONSE_TOO_LARGE` with `retry: false`, and the hint names the key to raise; the other
  calls in flight keep their retryable error. The owner is read from the start of the cut message
  (its `id` before its `result` or `error`); when that cannot be read, nobody is told the answer
  was too large.
