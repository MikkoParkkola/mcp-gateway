- **Activity outcome counts.** `GET /ui/api/activity/counts` answers how many calls the Activity list shows for each Outcome value (all, ok, failed, blocked and cleaned) under the list's own filters and window, numbers only. `from` and `to` are required; any parameter the list's filters do not include is refused with 400 by name. The counts are read in one bounded pass over the logs: `complete` is true only when the read reached the logs' start, and `stopped` says why it ended (`end`, `window_passed` or `cap`), with the counts read so far returned either way. A log that rotates during the read answers 503. Counts reads hold at most two of the four Activity read slots and stop within one pass when their caller goes away.
