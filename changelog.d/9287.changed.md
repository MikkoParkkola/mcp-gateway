- **A stdio backend now gets to exit on its own when the gateway stops it.** The gateway used to close a stdio backend's stdin and kill its process group at once, so a backend that saves state when its input closes lost that save on every stop. That included a gateway run as another's stdio backend, which lost its final telemetry, cost, task and audit writes. It now follows the MCP stdio shutdown order: close stdin, wait for the backend to exit, send SIGTERM to its process group if it has not, wait again, then kill the group. The waits fit the stop's own budget: at shutdown, `server.shutdown_timeout` (by default 16 s after stdin closes and 8 s after SIGTERM, with 6 s kept for the kill); on an eviction or a restart, its 10 s close budget. A backend that exits when its stdin closes costs no extra time. A backend judged dead or unresponsive (a failed health probe, a failed start or handshake, a call that timed out, a revoked capability) is still killed at once. A restart's close of the old backend is now bounded at 10 s. (MIK-9287)
