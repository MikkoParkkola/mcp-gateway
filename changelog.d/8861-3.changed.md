- **`auth.api_keys` applies on reload.** A key added, removed, edited or expired by a config reload is judged by the next request, and by open streams, dashboard sessions, watch polls and queued tasks at their next delivery, poll or dispatch; spent rate-limit usage carries over. A reload whose keys would change multi-user status, collide with the running bearer, or name an unresolvable secret reference is refused whole, on stdio too, and the running keys stay in force. Every other `auth` setting still needs a restart. (MIK-8861)
