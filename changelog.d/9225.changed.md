- **A refused key revocation says why, where, and how to finish by hand.** When the in-place editor cannot remove a key's entry from the config file, `DELETE /ui/api/keys/{name}` still answers `409` with `"error": "not_written"` and `"revoked": false`, and now also carries `code` (the editor's cause, for example `anchor` or `flow`), `reason`, and `path` (the config file to edit). When the entry is one the editor's revocation check refuses (an anchor, an alias or flow style), the answer adds `instructions`: the expiry-first steps to revoke the key by hand, word for word the same as `reason`. Every other refusal keeps its previous answer.
