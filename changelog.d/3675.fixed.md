- **A withheld question now gives its continuation slot back on streams and stdio too.** Continuing MIK-8176: a question the gateway withheld after sealing it (a cross-tenant read blocked, a delivery record that could not be written, a failed grant-decision write) now frees its continuation slot when the client asked over `POST /mcp` with an event stream, or over stdio, not only on plain JSON and `POST /mcp/{name}`. On stdio, an answer still waiting to be written when the session ends also gives its slot back. Delivered questions keep their slot for the client's retry. Affects 4.0.0 pre-releases only (MIK-8176).
