- **An allowed call's decision record names the rule that allowed it.** Until now every allowed call recorded `default_allow`, whichever check let it through. The record now names the most specific rule that allowed the call, by kind and keyed rule id: a matched identity grant (with the operator's `grant_id`), then an mTLS rule, then a tool-policy allow-list entry, then a firewall rule. A firewall rule that softens a blocking finding to allow outranks a grant. A call no rule allowed, or one allowed only by a policy's default action, still records `default_allow`. Listings and silent rechecks note nothing. (MIK-9205)
