- **Each call's decision record lists what the request firewall found in that call's own arguments.** The new `request_findings` list in the signed decision record names each finding's scan type, severity and where it was found, never the matched text or its description. It keeps the first 16 distinct findings and sets `request_findings_truncated` when more were dropped. A cache hit or an idempotency replay scans its own arguments for the record without changing the answer. A call refused before the firewall ran, or one made with the firewall switched off, has no list; a scan that found nothing writes an empty one. A warning that only the route stage raised on a call it let through stays in the firewall's audit log.
