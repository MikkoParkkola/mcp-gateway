- **Activity: a receipt for each call, and a list that keeps its place.** Opening an Activity row shows that entry's receipt: who made the call, the decision and the rule it matched, the answer's effect, and its place in the signed chain. Only the fields the receipt lists are shown, and Copy as JSON copies exactly those (a read-only selected box stands in when the browser refuses the clipboard). A row whose delivery is not yet recorded says so and offers Check again. A recorded rule links to the list filtered by that rule, with a Clear button. The list reads the next page when its end comes into view. While you read older pages, live news no longer replaces them: a "Newer activity · show" button waits at the top and brings back the newest page. Leaving the screen or changing a filter cancels the request still out. A failed refresh keeps the rows and says the newest calls could not be read.
