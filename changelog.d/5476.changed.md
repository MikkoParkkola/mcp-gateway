- **"Blocked" means a rule refused the call, everywhere.** A call is blocked when a policy, a scope or grant check, or the firewall refused it: its audit record says `denied`. An invalid call (an unknown tool name, arguments that do not parse) is now counted and listed as failed, not blocked: Activity's `outcome=blocked` and `outcome=failed` filters, their counts and both exports move it from the first to the second, matching the counters, which already counted it as an error. A response the firewall refuses is now recorded as `denied` with code -32600 and counted as a block and a firewall catch on both routes; before, the direct route recorded it as an error and `/mcp` recorded the call as `ok`. Graceful shutdown now waits, within its drain window, for audit records still being written for requests the client dropped, and logs any it could not wait for.
  Affects: anyone filtering Activity by outcome, or reading the blocked and error counters, who relied on invalid calls under blocked or on refused answers under ok or error. Operator action: look for invalid calls under failed.
