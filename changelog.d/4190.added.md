- **`keychain:` references read on Linux and macOS.** On Linux, through the Secret Service: a locked or missing entry is refused without spawning `secret-tool`, so no unlock dialog can open, and a missing `gdbus` or `secret-tool` is refused naming its package. On macOS, through `security-framework`, with keychain prompts switched off for the whole process; a call that would need a dialog fails as locked. Each read is bounded at 2 s, and its bytes are used exactly. (MIK-8908)
