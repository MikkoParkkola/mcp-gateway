- **Signing refusals on `/mcp` and stdio now leave an audit record, and replays and chain failures name their own check.** A `tools/call` refused for its signing nonce (malformed, missing where one is required, already used, or over the store's capacity) used to be answered with no invocation record on the meta transports. It now writes one record before the answer, with the refusal's code and outcome, and is counted like any refused call. The same holds for a call the invocation policy refuses at signing time (for example a backend the operator has killed), which records `denied` with its kind, as it does when refused at dispatch; under a fail-closed audit log a refusal whose record cannot be written is answered as the log being unavailable. Two decision kinds are new: `replay_guard`, for a single-use token the gateway turned away (a nonce seen before or refused for capacity, a continuation that is spent, forged or expired), and `signature_chain`, for an upstream reply whose signature chain did not verify. Both used to record `unrecorded`. A nonce that is not a string, or two nonces on one call, records `syntax`. The answers callers receive are unchanged. (MIK-9219)
