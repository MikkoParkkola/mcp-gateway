- The gateway now keeps call history for the web UI's charts: calls, blocks, errors, firewall catches and cache hits in half-hour buckets over 48 hours and in host-local days over 30 days, overall and per server, tool, caller and caller-and-server. Each call counts once, refusals included, whether or not the audit log is on. Restarts, upgrades, config reloads, breaker pauses and kill-switch changes are kept as markers. Both survive a restart in `counters.json` beside `costs.json`. Admins read them at `GET /ui/api/metrics/series` and `GET /ui/api/markers`. Only configured servers, catalogued tools and verified callers get their own line; any other name a caller sends is only counted, never stored. (MIK-8854)
