- **`audit verify` pairs config-write records.** Once the chain check passes, `audit verify` pairs each settle record (a `settle.`-prefixed `kind`) with its intent by token and role. The run fails with "Pairing verification FAILED", naming the rule, the counter and the token but never a payload value, in four cases: a dependent record names no intent of its token, one token and role hold two different records, a `config_write_delivery_stopped` record has no outcome, or a settle record lacks a field its role requires. An intent with no terminal record is a warning: "interrupted", or "left to recovery" after a deferral. A record naming an intent that retention deleted is an info line. When the chain check fails, pairing findings print as "unverified:" warnings and never change the verdict. Intents written before outcome records existed are counted in one info line and not paired. `VerifyResult` gains `failed_check` (which check failed) and `notes` (the info lines).
