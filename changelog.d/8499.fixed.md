- A refused or served meta-tool call (`gateway_invoke` and other meta tools) no longer copies its whole argument tree to carry the client's `params._meta` into it: intake moves `_meta` into the arguments in place. On the router test fixture (input sanitizer off, firewall off, the body in one chunk), a refused 1 MiB call now allocates its parse plus about 47 KB, down from about 3.2 MB more; Known limit in 4.0.0: `_meta` itself is still copied once, so a client that puts its bulk in `params._meta` rather than `arguments` still costs a copy of it (MIK-8499; follow-up MIK-8536).
