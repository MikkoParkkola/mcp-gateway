- A refused or served meta-tool call (`gateway_invoke` and other meta tools) no longer copies its whole argument tree to carry the client's `params._meta` into it: intake moves `_meta` into the arguments in place. On the router test fixture (input sanitizer off, firewall off, the body in one chunk), a refused 1 MiB call now allocates its parse plus about 47 KB, down from about 3.2 MB more; `_meta` itself is still copied once (MIK-8499).
