- **Load more and Keep looking on Activity.** The call list ends with one line that says how to get more: a Load more button while older calls exist, "Reading more…" while they are fetched, and Retry if that fetch fails. When the gateway stopped searching before the end of the log, the line says so and offers Keep looking instead of claiming there are no calls, and the screen waits for that press rather than reading further on its own. Both buttons work from the keyboard, keep focus in the list, and say what they found to screen readers.
