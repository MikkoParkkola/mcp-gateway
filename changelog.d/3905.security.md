- **An open `tools/list_changed` stream re-checks its agent, certificate and proxy at every notification.** A GET or `subscriptions/listen` stream held under an OAuth agent is told what that agent's current registry scopes show, not what they showed at attach. It closes when its bearer validates as another agent, when its client certificate is past `notAfter` (or the clock cannot be read), or when its trusted proxy is no longer in `trusted_proxies`. Each closure is counted once in `mcp_view_liveness_dead_total{reason}` as `agent`, `cert` or `proxy`. (MIK-8377)
  Affects: deployments with agent auth, mTLS client certificates or `trusted_proxy` identity that hold streams open.
  Operator action: none; clients reconnect. To end a revoked certificate's open stream, restart with the new CRL or issue short-lived certificates.
