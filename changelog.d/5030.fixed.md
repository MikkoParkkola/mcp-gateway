- **A healthy HTTP stop under load no longer exits 75.** The gateway waited at most a quarter of a second for its last blocking work and reported anything longer as work left running, so on a busy host a clean stop exited 75 and its supervisor marked it failed. The stop now waits for that work until its configured stop deadline. Under load, or with a write that never returns, a stop can therefore take up to that deadline instead of exiting 75 after a quarter of a second; a late write now completes instead of being cut off. Work still running at the deadline is left behind with exit 75 as before, and the stop logs one line naming how long it waited and what was still alive.
