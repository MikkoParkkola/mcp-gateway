- **A tools/list fill cut short no longer grants cache admission or resend.** A fill that stops early (at 32 pages, at 120 s, on a repeated cursor, on an unreadable page) may not have reached a later listing of the same tool without the hint. Such a fill now only narrows the read-only and resend sets: a tool keeps cache admission and resend only if it already held them and the pages read still declare it. A complete fill still grants both. (MIK-9161)
