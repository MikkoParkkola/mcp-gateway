- The response cache stores and serves a result only for a tool declared read-only: `readOnlyHint: true` in a backend's `tools/list`, or `read_only: true` in a capability's YAML. A capability's own `cache:` is ignored, with a warning at load, unless it is declared read-only. A repeated write within the cache's lifetime was answered from the cache and never ran, so the caller was told it succeeded. A call to a removed backend could also get its old cached answer. Reported with a fix in #3773 by Justin Wood.
