- **Policy hits per rule.** `GET /ui/api/policies` now gives every finding rule, tool-policy deny, allow and built-in deny its `rule_id` (the id decision records carry), `hits_24h` and `hits_prev_24h` (how often it decided a call in the last 24 hours and in the 24 hours before) and `exceptions` (a finding rule's `except` entries; the allows that wholly or partly override a deny). A top-level `hits` object states the two windows, whose calls were counted, and whether the count is `complete`. The counts come from one bounded read of the activity log under the caller's Activity access. A read that stops early returns the counts it read with `complete` false and the reason in `stopped` (`window_passed` or `cap`). With no log, a clock before 1970, a log that rotated during the read or a read that failed, the rules are still served and every count is null.
