- **`tools/list` no longer stalls after a failed per-user credential lookup.** On a backend with per-user sessions only, a cold caller's listing claims a fill of the caller's own tool list. When that claim's credential lookup failed, its lease stayed as the retry backoff, and the caller's listings inside that backoff waited for it as if a fill were running: up to the backend's `timeout` plus a short grace (at most 30 s), and then answered without that backend's tools anyway. A listing now waits only while a lookup or fill is really running, and a listing already waiting is woken as soon as the attempt fails. The retry backoff itself is unchanged: nothing is fetched for that caller until it ends. (MIK-9006)
