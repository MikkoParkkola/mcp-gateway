- **The library's reload entries ask the restart gate.** `ReloadContext::reload` and `ReloadContext::reload_outcome` take the restart gate's admission before reloading gateway.yaml, and return its refusal text while a restart is being prepared, restarting or stopping; their write-and-reload and mutate-and-reload entries also write nothing then. The identity-grants reload stays ungated, so a revocation applies at once. See `docs/UPGRADING-4.0.md`.
