- **`gateway_list_servers` and `gateway_get_stats` no longer report the capability count as known while the startup scan runs.** An HTTP gateway answers before its capability directories are loaded, and `/readyz` waits for that scan; until now the capabilities row said `tools_known: true` with the count of a partial catalogue, often `0`, so an agent could give up on a backend that was only loading. The row now reports `tools_count: null` and `tools_known: false` until the scan completes, as a registry backend's row does. `gateway_get_stats` reports `tools_known: false` meanwhile; its `tools_available` stays a number, a floor while `tools_known` is false, and its description says so.
