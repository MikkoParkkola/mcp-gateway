- **A backend registered before the events startup check keeps its subscriptions.** At startup the events hub withdraws the subscriptions of backends removed while the gateway was down. A config reload that registered a backend in the middle of that check could have the backend's stored subscriptions withdrawn although it was registered by then. A registration now waits for the check to finish: a backend registered before the check keeps its subscriptions, and one registered after it finds the subscriptions of a backend absent at startup already withdrawn. Affects 4.0.0 pre-releases only (MIK-7897).
