- **`VerifiedIdentity` has read-only accessors.** The library's `key_server::oidc::VerifiedIdentity` now offers `subject()`, `email()`, `name()`, `groups()` and `issuer()`, and the gateway reads an identity only through them. The type and its checked constructor moved into their own module, re-exported at the same path; its fields are unchanged in this release step. Library users can switch field reads to the accessors now: a later change makes the fields private. (MIK-9207)
