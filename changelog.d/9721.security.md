- **Short credentials are flagged, and refused under the hardened posture.** A literal or `env:` `auth.bearer_token` under 20 characters logs one warning at load, naming the field and the floor but never the value; under `security.posture: hardened` it refuses the load and names the fix (20 or more characters, or `auto`). `auto`, `file:` and `keychain:` tokens are not measured. `mcp-gateway hash-key` refuses a new key under 20 bytes; `--verify` still checks a shorter key. The "Invalid token" warning carries the peer IP of the connection, never an address from a forwarding header.
  Affects: 4.0.0 pre-releases and earlier. Operator action: under `security.posture: hardened`, set `auth.bearer_token` to 20 or more characters, or `auto`, before upgrading.
