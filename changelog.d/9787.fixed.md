- **A container stop no longer loses the gateway's records.** The gateway saves its state about 20 ms after a stop signal and every second after that, so a kill at the end of a container's 10 s stop grace loses at most what was recorded since the last save landed: about a second, plus the time one write takes. Open event streams end at the signal instead of holding the stop for the listener grace. `usage.json`, `transitions.json` and the tool-profile file are now replaced by rename, so a kill during a save leaves the old file or the new one.
