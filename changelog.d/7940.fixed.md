- **A backend that comes back is listened to again at once.** When a backend was removed and re-added (or replaced by a config reload) just as its event listener found it gone, or while the listener was waiting to reconnect, the listener could miss the signal and wait up to 30 s, or its full reconnect backoff, before listening again. It now reconnects at once. Changes to subscriptions alone still leave a failing backend's reconnect backoff in place. Affects 4.0.0 pre-releases only (MIK-7940).
- **An owner-scoped event is delivered after its record expires.** An owner-scoped event, such as a task's final status after the task record expired, is no longer refused and its subscription revoked by the delivery worker. Affects 4.0.0 pre-releases only (MIK-7940).
