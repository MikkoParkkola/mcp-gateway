- Redacting a large tool answer is fast again. The 4.0.0 fix that keeps a second redaction pass from changing anything made redaction of a JSON array near the 1 MiB cap about three times slower than before it. That cost is gone: such an answer now takes about 2.5 times the plain whole-text redaction, against 8 to 9 times, and plain redaction is no slower than before. Redacted output is unchanged byte for byte. (MIK-8896)
