- An upstream event listen that the backend answers with `-32601` or HTTP 405 now ends as unsupported on HTTP, stdio and WebSocket, instead of being retried fast as a failure. On a modern stream, notices that arrive before the acknowledgement, or fall outside what it honoured, are dropped; notices still being coalesced when a session ends are delivered. Affects 4.0.0 pre-releases only (MIK-7899, MIK-7898).
