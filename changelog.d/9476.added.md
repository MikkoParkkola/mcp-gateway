- **Web UI: deny an open access request.** `POST /ui/api/decisions/{id}/resolve` with `action: deny` records one governance audit event: action `mutate_grant`, the administrator as actor, their reason, and a `ui_decision` body naming the subject, the agent, the scope digest they were shown and the exact refusals it closes. The request leaves the To do queue at once and stays closed after a restart; a later refusal of the same capability opens it again, and it can be denied again. A retry with the same digest answers the recorded outcome and writes nothing. The caller stays refused. **Library:** `ControlPlaneAuditEvent` gains the optional `ui_decision` field, with the new non-exhaustive types `UiDecisionRecord` and `UiDecisionEntry`. (MIK-9476)
