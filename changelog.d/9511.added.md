- **Web UI: Gateway › Settings lists unused secret entries.** The new UI's Gateway page opens on its Settings tab, which lists the entries a failed or rolled-back save left in the files store or the keychain (name, store, when written) and any store that could not be read, and deletes one entry at a time after a confirm. A refused delete shows the gateway's own reason and the entry stays listed; a delete the gateway could not confirm says so with the gateway's reason and offers a reload. Status and Actions on this page come later. (MIK-9511)
