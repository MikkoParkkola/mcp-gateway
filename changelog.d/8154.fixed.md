- **A keyed `/mcp/{name}` call now shares the execution lease with `/mcp`, stdio and tasks.** One idempotency key covers one backend operation, whichever route sends it. Before, the direct route checked only its idempotency cache. A direct call under the key of a task or a `gateway_invoke` still in flight could reach the backend a second time, and a retry after the cache entry expired ran the call again. Now a direct call whose key belongs to another execution or representation is refused with 409 "Idempotency key belongs to another execution or representation", and the backend runs once. A completed direct call retried after its cache entry expires replays the stored answer, signed for the retry's own nonce. A refused call gives its cache reservation back and spends no signing nonce. (MIK-8154)
  Affects: deployments whose clients send idempotency keys to `/mcp/{name}`.
  Operator action: none.
