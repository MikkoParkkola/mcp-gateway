change: The gateway stops a stdio backend by closing its stdin, waiting for it to exit, then SIGTERM, then SIGKILL, within the stop's budget, instead of killing its process group at once

## Stdio backends get the MCP shutdown order

**Startup:** no notice, a log warning when a backend needs SIGTERM or the kill

What you gain: a stdio backend that saves state when its input closes (a
gateway run as another's stdio backend among them) keeps those writes on
every stop, eviction and requested restart.

What you keep: a backend judged dead or unresponsive (failed health probe,
failed start or handshake, timed-out call, revoked capability) is killed at
once, as before.

What changes:

- At shutdown the waits come from `server.shutdown_timeout` (default 30 s):
  16 s after stdin closes, 8 s after SIGTERM, 6 s kept for the kill. A
  backend that exits on EOF costs only its own exit time.
- On an eviction or a requested restart the budget is the 10 s close budget:
  about 2.7 s after stdin closes and 1.3 s after SIGTERM.
- The gateway logs a warning naming the backend when it has to send SIGTERM,
  and when it has to kill the group.
