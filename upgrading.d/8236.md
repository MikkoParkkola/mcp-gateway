## Firewall audit rows no longer record what a scan matched

**Startup:** no notice, a change to what each audit row records rather than to startup

What you gain: the firewall audit log (`security.firewall.audit_log`) holds no
request or response content. Each finding is written as its `scan_type`,
`severity` and `location`, and every row carries `"schema_version": 3`
(request and dispatch rows too; response rows were version 2).

What you lose: a finding's `matched` excerpt (up to 200 characters of what
the scan matched) and its `description` (which named the argument's key) are
no longer written. The refusal a client receives and the gateway's own
in-process logs are unchanged.

What to do about logs you already have: rows written before this upgrade may
contain fragments of request arguments, of backend responses, and argument
key names. Treat those files, and their backups, as sensitive: restrict
access, and rotate or purge them if your retention policy requires it. To find
the affected rows, look for any row without `"schema_version": 3` that has
findings:

```sh
jq -c 'select((.schema_version // 0) < 3 and (.findings | length) > 0)' audit.jsonl
```

A log parser or SIEM rule that reads `findings[].matched` or
`findings[].description` switches to `scan_type`, `severity` and `location`.
