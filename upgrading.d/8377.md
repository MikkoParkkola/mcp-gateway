change: An open `tools/list_changed` stream (`GET /mcp` or `subscriptions/listen`) re-checks its OAuth agent, client certificate and trusted proxy at every notification: the agent's current registry scopes decide what it is told, and the stream closes when its bearer validates as another agent, the certificate's `notAfter` has passed (or the clock cannot be read), or the proxy is no longer in `trusted_proxies`; a stream dead at attach is refused with 401 "Invalid token"; closures count in `mcp_view_liveness_dead_total{reason}`
action: Reconnect a client whose stream closed after its certificate expired or its agent changed; issue short-lived client certificates, or restart with the new CRL, to end a revoked certificate's open stream

## Open `list_changed` streams re-check agent, certificate and proxy

**Startup:** no notice

- What you lose: an open stream no longer outlives its client certificate's `notAfter`, a change of
  the OAuth agent its bearer validates as, or its proxy's removal from `trusted_proxies`. It closes,
  and the client must reconnect with an identity that holds. A stream whose identity is already dead
  when it opens is refused with 401 "Invalid token", as an invalid credential is.
- What stays the same: a stream whose identity still holds is told exactly as before. Certificate
  revocation is still checked only at the TLS handshake (the CRL is read at startup).
- What you gain: an agent narrowed in the registry is told only about tools it can still see, and no
  stream keeps an identity that has lapsed. `mcp_view_liveness_dead_total{reason}` counts each
  closure under `agent`, `cert` or `proxy`.
- What you do: nothing, unless a client treats a closed stream as fatal. It should reconnect.
