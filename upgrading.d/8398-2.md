## Meta-tool provenance receipt moves to the outer result

**Startup:** no notice

Before, on `/mcp`, a meta-tool reply carried its `_meta.provenance` receipt inside `content[0].text`, pretty-printed with the wrapped result. After, the receipt sits in the outer `result._meta.provenance`, and `content[0].text` carries the result without it. Read the receipt from `result._meta.provenance`.

Chain and playbook step receipts stay inside the parsed `content[0].text`, at `results[i].result._meta.provenance` and `output.<step>._meta.provenance`.
