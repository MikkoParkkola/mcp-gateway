## The probed-peer era is PeerEra

**Startup:** no notice

**What you gain.** The type's name says what it is: what a backend was probed to speak; protocol::meta::Era (what a request declared) keeps its name.

**What you lose.** The old path `mcp_gateway::protocol::era::Era` no longer resolves, so a `use` of it fails to compile.

**What you do.** Replace `mcp_gateway::protocol::era::Era` with `mcp_gateway::protocol::era::PeerEra`. The variants `Modern`/`Legacy` and their serialized strings `modern`/`legacy` are unchanged.
