## The relay counter gains a `reason` label

**Startup:** no notice

**What you lose.** A dashboard or alert that reads
`mcp_gateway_collusion_relay_total{action="block"}` without aggregating now gets one series per
reason instead of one per action.

**What you gain.** Each relay refusal is counted under why it was reported: `relay` (plain
detection), `other_source` (the caller holds the text from a different tool), `capacity` (a
detector capacity limit decided the label) or `unkeyed` (no authenticated caller). The new
`mcp_gateway_collusion_capacity_total{bound}` shows when a capacity limit is hit: `bound` is one
of `receipt_truncated`, `fingerprint_evicted`, `record_dropped`, `record_replaced`,
`record_overflow`, `sketch_refused`, `sketch_evicted`, `marker_evicted` or `record_text_cut`.

**What you do.** Aggregate over `reason`:
`sum by (action) (rate(mcp_gateway_collusion_relay_total[5m]))`. No shipped alert or doc query
reads this series.
