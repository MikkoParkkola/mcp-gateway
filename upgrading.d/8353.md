## A refused signed task call gets its nonce back when nothing was confirmed

**Startup:** no notice

**A task call refused before any confirmation gets its nonce back.**
- What you gain: a signed, task-augmented call that execution admission refuses
  (a killed backend, a target policy, a plan check), when no destructive
  confirmation was spent for it, gives its signing nonce back. The honest call
  re-sent under that nonce is judged on its merits instead of being refused as a
  replay. This is the rule plain calls already followed since 4.0.0 pre-releases.
- What stays the same: a task call refused after its destructive confirmation was
  accepted keeps its nonce spent, so the same signed request can never be
  replayed. Re-send it under a fresh nonce. A task the task store refuses at
  submit (a key reused for other arguments, a key still in flight, the store at
  capacity, a sealed run) also keeps its nonce spent for now; re-send those
  under a fresh nonce too.

**A refused destructive task needs a new confirmation.**
- What stays the same: a destructive task you confirmed, and that the gateway then
  refuses before running it (a nonce replay, an admission refusal or any later
  check before dispatch), has used its confirmation. A confirmation authorises one
  attempt, not one success. Re-send the call and confirm again.
- What you do: nothing as an operator. A client that retries a refused destructive
  task answers the new confirmation question.
