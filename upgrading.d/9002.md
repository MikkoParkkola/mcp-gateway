action: Read `_recovery`, `_predicted_next` and `_trace_id` instead of the plain names

## The gateway's hint, prediction and trace id carry a `_` prefix

**Startup:** no notice

Before 4.0 the gateway wrote its recovery hint, its next-call prediction and
the call's trace id under `recovery`, `predicted_next` and `trace_id`. A
backend may use those names for its own data, so the gateway could not tell
its own members from a backend's copy, and a backend could pass off its own
advice, prediction or correlation id as the gateway's.

What changes for clients:

- **Read the `_` names.** The gateway writes `_recovery`, `_predicted_next`
  and `_trace_id`, and removes a backend's copy of them before the answer
  reaches you. A native answer carries them at its top level; a task carries
  them at the top level of its stored result (the task envelope's `result`);
  a wrapped `gateway_invoke` carries them inside the JSON in
  `content[0].text`.
- **The plain names are backend data.** A `recovery`, `predicted_next` or
  `trace_id` in an answer is whatever the backend sent; do not treat a plain
  name on a newer answer as the gateway's. A backend's own `recovery` on an
  error result no longer stops the gateway from adding `_recovery`.
- **Tasks settled before the upgrade keep the plain names** in their stored
  results and are delivered without `_` members.
- **Nested `_` members inside composite step results are not stripped and
  must not be trusted by clients.** Only the top level of a result is
  judged; a chain's or playbook's per-step results are backend data.
- **A result stored before 4.0 that carried backend-planted gateway members
  is delivered without them and without its public signature; the internal
  receipt remains.** A client that verifies provenance signatures sees such a
  result unsigned rather than failing verification.

Not changed: the gateway log's `trace_id`, the audit record's `trace_id` and
the tracing span field keep their names.

Limit: a gateway-only member the gateway writes without noting it
(`_context_integrity`, the task outcome and reason under `_meta`, a tool's
`trustCard`) is delivered as stored, so a copy planted in a row stored before
4.0's ingress strip survives until that member is noted where it is written.
