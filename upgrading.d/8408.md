## A replayed plan renews its relay receipts

**Startup:** no notice

What you gain: with relay detection on, re-reading a stored plan result no
longer lets its text be relayed unseen once the detector's window (600 s) has
passed. A re-read renews the reader's receipts for what it delivers, each step
under its own source.

What changes:

- Renewal copies what the live delivery recorded, never more. The gateway's own
  text, a step a replay-time check redacted, and an unauthorized read renew
  nothing.
- The renewal data lives in the gateway's memory only, budgeted at 1 MiB per
  stored answer and 32 MiB in total, oldest dropped first. The budget counts
  fingerprint and text bytes plus a per-item estimate, so actual memory use can
  run somewhat above it (exact accounting: MIK-8550). Nothing is written to disk.
- Effective ceiling: a keyed sync replay serves at most 512 KiB (fixed) and a
  task re-read at most `tasks.max_record_bytes` (default 512 KiB). At default
  settings no replayed plan exceeds 512 KiB; the 1 MiB entry cap applies only
  to a task re-read with `tasks.max_record_bytes` raised.
- Limits, each failing toward a missed renewal: a plan read after a gateway
  restart, an entry evicted under the budget or over the entry cap, a plan
  delivered while relay detection was off, and the seams of a plan with a
  nested step do not renew.
- Two counters on the metrics endpoint:
  `mcp_gateway_collusion_plan_replay_skipped_total` (by reason) and
  `mcp_gateway_collusion_plan_replay_evicted_total`.

**Rollback:** no format change. The task record stays at the version this
release already writes, so rolling back follows the 4.0.0 procedure unchanged.
