## The telemetry cache scope is ShadowCacheScope

**Startup:** no notice

**What you gain.** The type's name says what it is: a shadow-metrics value that can say `public`, unlike protocol::cacheable::CacheScope, which keeps its name.

**What you lose.** The old path `mcp_gateway::protocol_revision_telemetry::CacheScope` no longer resolves, so a `use` of it fails to compile.

**What you do.** Replace `mcp_gateway::protocol_revision_telemetry::CacheScope` with `mcp_gateway::protocol_revision_telemetry::ShadowCacheScope`. The variants and their `as_str()` strings `public`/`private` are unchanged.
