## An expired continuation retry answers the continuation error, never a retained result

**Startup:** no notice

**What changes.** A keyed continuation retry whose envelope has expired (or is otherwise unreadable) is answered with the continuation's own error, even when a retained result for its key is present. Previously the retained result was served.
