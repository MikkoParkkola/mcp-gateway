## Signed calls to a per-user backend are checked after the nonce until the caller's list is read

**Startup:** no notice

What you gain: on stdio with message signing, a tool your own list on a
per-user backend hides can no longer be told apart from a missing one. Before,
when other callers had pushed that tool into error-rate cooldown, the call
answered "temporarily disabled due to a high error rate" and named it, while a
missing name answered "Unknown tool". Both now answer -32601 "Unknown tool",
and nothing is sent.

What changes for a caller who can see the tool. This covers a signed
`gateway_invoke` to a per-user backend on stdio always, and on `/mcp` when the
gateway could not read your tool list or could not prove it complete:

- The invocation policy is checked at dispatch, after the nonce, not before
  it. A replayed nonce on a tool in cooldown is refused as a replay, not with
  the cooldown text.
- A call refused for the cooldown keeps its nonce spent. Retry with a fresh
  nonce.
- On signed stdio the cooldown refusal now reads `JSON-RPC error -32000:
  Capability '<tool>' on server '<server>' is temporarily disabled ...`, the
  text unsigned stdio already answers.

What stays the same: `/mcp` with a tool list the gateway has read and proven
complete checks the policy before the nonce, as before, and its cooldown text
keeps no prefix. Shared (not per-user) backends are unchanged.

What to do:

- Nothing, to keep working.
- Send a fresh nonce when you retry a cooldown refusal.
- If a client matches the signed stdio cooldown text exactly, match the
  prefixed form.
