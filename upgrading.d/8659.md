## A bound anonymous caller gets continuations on the direct route

**Startup:** no notice

**What you gain.** An anonymous caller that carries a passthrough identity binding
now receives continuations on the direct route (POST /mcp/{name}), as on the meta
route, charged to the shared anonymous/public quota.

**What you lose.** Nothing. Unbound anonymous callers are still refused on both routes.

**What you do.**

- Nothing.
