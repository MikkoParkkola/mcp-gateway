## A follow-up question the content gate blocks ends the call

**Startup:** no notice

What you gain: a question the gate judged unsafe to show is never resumed
behind the client's back. A task used to call the backend again with no
answer and report success; a direct call handed back a resume handle for an
exchange the client could not see.

What changes: such a call ends with JSON-RPC error -32600 "Response blocked by
security firewall", on every route (direct, task, chain, playbook). A task ends
`failed` with that error. In a playbook the step fails and is not retried;
`on_error` decides what happens next.
