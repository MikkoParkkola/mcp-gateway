## Delivery records name the credential, as invocation records do

**Startup:** no notice

**What you gain.** A delivery record now says which credential was presented, not
only the client's name. An API key named `bearer`, `dashboard`, `anonymous` or
`public` was indistinguishable from the built-in identity of that name in a
delivery record. Now `who.credential_kind` (`api_key`, `static_bearer`,
`oidc_bearer`, `key_server_token`, `dashboard_session`, `local_transport`, or
`none`) and `who.principal` tell them apart, as invocation records already did
(this item's earlier text said every record carries them; delivery records did
not).

**What stays the same.** `caller`, `who.account`, `who.authority` and
`who.subject` are byte-identical. No new field appears in the firewall audit
log, and no credential digest is added beyond the existing `who.principal`.
