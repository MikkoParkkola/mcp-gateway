## Relay detection now covers plan answers over 1 MiB

**Startup:** no notice

- What you lose: a relay of text from a plan whose answer is over 1 MiB, which used to pass
  unseen, is now refused under `block` (403, "Relay detection blocked") and reported under
  `observe`. A caller whose plan answer cannot be read (over 4 MiB of text, past the view's
  entry cap, or with no room in the transient budget) is refused for lost room when it relays
  that plan's text.
- What you gain: the holder of such a plan is no longer refused for text the answer delivered
  to it. The answer is read through a bounded sampled view, and every byte it uses (the view, its
  walks, the retained cuts) is reserved from a 32 MiB transient budget before it is allocated, so
  a large answer cannot grow memory past it.
- What you do: nothing. Relay detection is off by default. New capacity counters:
  `bound="plan_answer_unread"`, `bound="plan_view_budget"`, `bound="plan_selection_budget"`,
  `bound="plan_view_cut_budget"` and `bound="plan_leaf_hashes_skipped"`.
