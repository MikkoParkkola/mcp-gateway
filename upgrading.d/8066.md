## Relay detection now sees the middle of a large delivery

**Startup:** no notice

- What you lose: a relay of text taken only from the middle of a delivery over 6 KiB, which
  used to pass unseen, is now refused under `block` (403, "Relay detection blocked") and
  reported under `observe`, like a relay of the head or tail.
- What you gain: the middle of a large delivery is evidence through a thinned sample, recorded
  under the receiving caller's own records, so that caller is still excused for its own copy.
  The miss rate grows with size: in a 64 KiB delivery a 500-char middle relay is missed about
  1 in 100,000 times; in a 1 MiB delivery, 78% of the time (before, always). Design §14.3 B1′.
- What you do: nothing. Relay detection is off by default; the per-delivery bound (4,096
  fingerprints) and the metrics are unchanged.
