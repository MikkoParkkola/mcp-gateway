## The redirect-chain checker is removed

**Startup:** no notice

**What you gain.** One redirect policy: the gateway's own fetches re-check every hop through the same SSRF check, and nothing public suggests a second one.

**What you lose.** The path `mcp_gateway::security::validate_redirect_chain` no longer resolves.

**What you do.** Call `mcp_gateway::security::validate_url_not_ssrf` on each hop's URL before you follow it.
