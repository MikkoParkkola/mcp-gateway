## A backend the routing profile leaves out answers as an unregistered one under mTLS or agent auth

**Startup:** no notice

- What changes: under an mTLS policy or agent auth, a backend the caller's routing profile leaves out is left out of that caller's listings, and a call naming it now answers exactly as a call naming a backend that does not exist.
  - On `POST /mcp` (`gateway_invoke` and the other meta-tools) 3.x refused it with "Backend '<name>' is not available in the '<profile>' routing profile", which told the caller the backend is registered.
  - On `/mcp/{name}` 3.x applied no routing profile and served the call. 4.0 applies the profile there; under an mTLS policy or agent auth its refusal is now the unregistered backend's answer too.
- What stays: with no mTLS policy and agent auth off, a call naming a backend the profile leaves out answers as it did before this change. A backend the profile admits answers as before.
