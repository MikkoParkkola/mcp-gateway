## `gateway_search` queries are capped at 256 characters

**Startup:** no notice

**What you gain.** One request can no longer make glob matching take
combinatorial time; the matcher is bounded and the query length is capped.

**What you lose.** A query over 256 characters (counted before lowercasing)
is refused with -32602 instead of searched. The message names the limit and
never echoes the query.

**What you do.** Shorten such queries. Every `gateway_search` query over 256
characters is refused, free text and glob patterns alike. No bundled
`server:tool` reference comes near that length (the test
`every_bundled_capability_reference_is_under_the_cap` checks it); references
from your own backends are not checked: one longer than 256 characters can
no longer be searched by its full name, so search it with a shorter glob such
as `server:prefix*`.
