change: Daily budgets reset at the host's local midnight instead of UTC midnight
action: None to upgrade. A host west or east of UTC sees its daily limits reset at its own midnight from the first UTC midnight after the upgrade

## Daily budgets count the host's local days

**Startup:** no notice, unless the host's time zone cannot be read: then one warning, and days count in UTC

**What changes.** The budget enforcer's daily limits (global, per tool and per key) reset when
the host's local date changes. On a clock-change day that is a 23- or 25-hour day. There is no
setting: the zone is the one the host names, read once at start, so a changed system zone takes
effect after a restart. The aggregate views of `/api/costs` and `/ui/api/costs` name it in
`day_zone`, for example `"Europe/Amsterdam"`, or `"UTC"` on the fallback.

**The first day after the upgrade.** A `costs.json` saved by an earlier version has no
`day_basis`. If it was saved on the current UTC day, its spend is restored and that day still
ends at UTC midnight, once. From then on days are local, and `costs.json` is saved with
`day_basis: "local"`. A snapshot from an earlier day is skipped, as before.

**What stays.** The rolling windows of per-key budgets (the last 24 hours, 7 days or 30 days)
are durations, not calendar days, and keep rolling.
