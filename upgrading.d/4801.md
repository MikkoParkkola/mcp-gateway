## Step seals: removed before a redacting egress firewall

**Startup:** no notice

**Who is affected.** Clients that verify `public_signature` (see `docs/provenance-public-key.md`) on chain or playbook step results, on `tasks/get` results and on task notifications, on a gateway whose firewall scans responses and has a redacting policy for the answer.

**Before.** A step result was sealed when the step answered. The egress firewall then redacted the delivered answer, but the step seal stayed, and its payload carried the digest of the unredacted step: a client could test guesses of the removed value offline, and the reference verifier answered `ResultMismatch`.

**After.** While response scanning is on and a redacting policy applies, every `public_signature` in the answer is removed before the scan, at any depth and in JSON text, even when nothing is redacted. The fresh top-level seal, made after the scan, covers what is delivered. An answer the firewall rewrote whose text names `public_signature` without parsing as JSON is refused. With no redacting policy, step seals stay and verify.

**What you do.** Nothing on the gateway. A verifier that requires a step-level signature treats a receipt without one as unsigned and relies on the top-level seal.
