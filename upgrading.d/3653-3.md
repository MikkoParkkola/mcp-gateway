## Audit records of CLI calls say how the child process ended

**Startup:** no notice

**What you lose.** Nothing. A record of a call that started no child is unchanged.

**What you gain.** An invocation record that ran a CLI child carries `process`: a list with one
entry per child, holding `ended` (`exited` with `exit_code`, `signalled`, `timed_out`,
`output_overflow` or `observation_failed`), `duration_ms` from spawn, and `stdout_bytes` and
`stderr_bytes` (null when the output was not kept). It holds counts only, never output, and at
most 16 entries, with `process_total` when more ran.

**What you do.** Nothing, unless a tool that reads the audit log refuses keys it does not
expect. Then allow `process` and `process_total`.
