## Public provenance signatures are available

**Startup:** refuses to start, only while `security.provenance_stamping` is on
and `GATEWAY_PROVENANCE_SIGNING_SEED` is set but malformed (not base64 of
exactly 32 bytes), its key id missing, malformed, or colliding with the
attestation key id.

What you gain: a stamped receipt or event also carries `public_signature`,
verifiable with the published JWK and no gateway secret. One exception in
4.0.0: a direct-route idempotent replay delivers its stored receipt without
`public_signature` (it was minted by the first call, so the replay does not
seal it); MIK-8705 adds the re-stamp.
Step receipts in chain and playbook answers carry the public signature.
`mcp-gateway provenance verify --trust <jwk-or-jwks> <receipt> [--result
<file>]` reports `BOUND`, `UNBOUND`, `SIGNATURE-ONLY` or `INVALID <reason>`.
See `docs/provenance-public-key.md` for the signed-bytes spec, the verifier
order, and rotation with an overlap window.

What to do:

- Optional. Leave both variables unset and nothing changes.
- To enable: generate `openssl rand -base64 32` into
  `GATEWAY_PROVENANCE_SIGNING_SEED` (an env file entry is fine), set
  `GATEWAY_PROVENANCE_KEY_ID` to 1-64 bytes of printable ASCII without
  spaces, restart, then publish the output of `mcp-gateway provenance
  public-key`.
