---
change: A tools/call whose backend asks the client a question keeps its idempotency key until the question is answered or expires
action: Answer the continuation, or rerun under a new idempotency key
---
## A call waiting on its question holds its key

**Startup:** no notice

**What you lose.** A same-key call that does not present the continuation's `requestState` no longer runs the tool again while the backend's question waits:

- while the continuation is live (up to 300 s), it is answered "Duplicate request in progress";
- once the continuation expires, it is answered the may-have-run notice.

This now covers a legacy exchange that minted a continuation (no session channel, or a bridged exchange that ran out of rounds), which used to release the key. A question the gateway could not hand back as a continuation (an anonymous caller, a question the client did not declare it can answer (`-32021`), a round a response gate replaced, a mint refused for want of a slot) keeps the key as may-have-run: a same-key retry gets the notice. A client refused with `-32021` that adds the capability and retries under the same key gets the notice, not its question.

**What you gain.** The tool runs once per key: a same-key retry during or after the question cannot run it a second time.

**What you do.** Answer the continuation with the `requestState` you were given. To run the call again instead, send it under a new idempotency key.
