## `--help` lists only the commands and flags 4.0 keeps

**Startup:** no notice

What you gain: a shorter `--help` that shows the commands you use. The
internal commands `kubernetes`, `ranking`, `runtime` and `trust`, and the
`-C/--capabilities` flag of `skills generate` and `tool invoke`, `tool list`,
`tool inspect` and `tool completions`, are no longer listed.

What changes: nothing a script sees. A hidden command still runs, and
`mcp-gateway kubernetes --help` still documents it. The `-C` flags still
parse. `add`, `remove`, `list` and `get` no longer declare their own
`--config/-c`; they read the global `--config` (or `MCP_GATEWAY_CONFIG`),
else `./gateway.yaml`, as they already did. `--config <file>` works before or
after the command name, and a flag still wins over the variable.

What to do: nothing.
