## `audit lookup-email`

**Startup:** no notice

`echo person@example.invalid | mcp-gateway audit lookup-email --email-stdin --url https://gateway.example.invalid` asks a running gateway's `POST /ui/api/key-server/audit/lookup-email` and prints the typed answer as JSON: the matching rows, `next_cursor`, `scan_exhausted`, `cursor_reset`, `unreadable` and `scope`. Page with `--cursor` and bound with `--limit`, `--from` and `--to`.

The email is never an argument (it would land in shell history and in every process listing), so there is no positional email and no email option: `--email-stdin` is required and reads one line, at most 1 KiB. The credential comes from `MCP_GATEWAY_TOKEN` and is never read from the command line. TLS flags are those of `dashboard-link`. A redirect is not followed, a cleartext non-loopback URL is refused, and nothing is written to disk. Any HTTP, authentication, read or decode failure is a nonzero exit; the command never prints the email or the credential in an error. The gateway's lookup budget applies (two lookups at once, then one every six seconds).
