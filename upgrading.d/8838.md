## ContinuationPurpose is non-exhaustive

**Startup:** no notice

**What you gain.** New continuation domains can be added in a minor release without breaking your build. The first one is `PreviewConfirm`, which seals the confirm step of the web UI's start-once preview (`POST /ui/api/previews`).

**What you lose.** An exhaustive `match` on `ContinuationPurpose` in your own crate no longer compiles: the compiler asks for a wildcard arm.

**What you do.** Add a `_ => …` arm to any `match` on `ContinuationPurpose`. Constructing `BackendInput`, `DestructiveConfirm` or `ChainResume`, and comparing purposes with `==`, keep working as before.
