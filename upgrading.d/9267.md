## Relay receipts: recorded when the answer is written

**Startup:** no notice

**Who is affected.** Deployments with `security.firewall.collusion.action` set to `observe` or `block`, or that switch it at runtime. Deployments that leave it `off` see no difference.

**Before.** An answer's relay receipts were recorded when the answer was ready, before it was written to stdout or the HTTP body was sent, under the setting in force at that moment. An answer prepared under `off` that went out after a switch to `block` was not recorded, so another caller could relay its text. In a stdio batch, an earlier item's receipt was recorded before the later items ran, so it excused a later item that passed the same text on.

**After.** Receipts record when stdout takes the frame, or when the server takes the first chunk of the HTTP body (the meta route, the direct route and request-scoped streams). The setting in force at that moment decides: under `off` nothing is recorded, even for an answer prepared under `block`. An answer that is never written, because the client went away or the session ended, records nothing. Each batch item records when the batch is written, so no item of a batch is excused by another item of the same batch: a client composes the whole batch before it receives any answer, so a later item cannot carry text the client got from an earlier one. A retained answer's replay snapshot is taken before the answer can be replayed, held while the answer is unsent, and kept or dropped by the setting at each write: one written only under `off` is not held once no delivery of it is pending. Task results keep their previous timing.

**What you do.** Nothing, unless a client relies on one batch item passing on text another caller holds that an earlier item of the same batch read. Send such items as separate requests, the later one after the earlier answer arrives.
