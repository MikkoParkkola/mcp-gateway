## `mcp_gateway::Error` is non-exhaustive

**Startup:** no notice, a library build that matches `Error` exhaustively no longer compiles

A refusal that a gateway decision makes (the kill switch, a capability disabled for its error rate, attestation, a spend limit) is now its own variant, `Error::GatewayRefused { code, message }`. It has the same code and text as the `Error::JsonRpc` it replaces. The enum is also marked `#[non_exhaustive]`, so later variants are not a breaking change.

- What you lose: an exhaustive `match` on `Error` from another crate; it needs a wildcard arm.
- What you gain: a refusal is told apart by its variant, not its text, and future variants do not break your build.
