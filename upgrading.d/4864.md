## One delivery window for the activity list, export and receipt

**Startup:** no notice

A delivery record (and a duplicate, poison, tombstone or untraceable-failure record)
now belongs to a call only when the bytes from the end of the call's line to the end of
that record are at most 256 KiB. The list, the export and the receipt apply the same
rule, and the list gives the same answer at every page size.

- The list response and the export manifest gain `delivery_beyond_window` and
  `delivery_window_bytes`.
- A call with no record inside the window reads `no_record`. After a finished search
  its finding says "no delivery record within 256 KiB after the call"; where no search
  finished the finding says "no delivery record found".
- Busy logs, where hundreds of other records sit between a call and its delivery, are
  the case where an effect that used to show can now read `no_record`.
