## Activity rule filter: refused calls listed from a grant decision are not matched

**Startup:** no notice

**Who is affected.** Operators who audit a firewall or grant rule through Activity's new `rule=` filter on the list or either export.

**Before.** Activity had no rule filter.

**After.** `rule=<id>` returns exactly the records whose decision object names that rule id. A call refused by the identity-grant check that has no invocation record is listed as a blocked row from its grant decision record. That row has no decision object with a rule id, so `rule=` never matches it, even when the refusal came from that rule.

**What to do.** To include those refusals, filter by `outcome=blocked` together with the caller, server or tool. The row's receipt shows the decision's reason and scope.
