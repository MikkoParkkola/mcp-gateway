## Watches and subscriptions close once after upgrading

**Startup:** no notice, each stored subscription and watch ends at its first check after the upgrade

What changes: a watch opened with an API key's secret lives only while
that secret is valid. A routine rotation keeps it until the grace for the
old secret ends; a rotation that drops the old secret at once (a key whose
secret leaked) ends it at its next check, through the web UI or after a
reload of an edited gateway.yaml.

What to do: once, after upgrading, reopen the event subscriptions and
watches your agents keep open. A client that reconnects when a watch ends
does this by itself.

When rotating a key by hand in gateway.yaml, keep its 'principal' (write
the 12 hex digits it has today) next to the previous digest and its expiry.
Without it the key derives a new principal from the new digest, and every
watch and dashboard session opened under it ends at once.
