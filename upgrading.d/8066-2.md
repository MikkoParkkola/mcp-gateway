## Relay detection now covers oversized plan steps

**Startup:** no notice

- What you lose: a relay of text from a plan step over 1 MiB, or from a step staged after its
  plan already held 1 MiB, which used to pass unseen, is now refused under `block` (403,
  "Relay detection blocked") and reported under `observe`.
- What you gain: the holder of such a step is no longer refused for relaying text it was itself
  delivered. The step keeps a bounded fingerprint sample and a compact filter in place of its
  text; in a 2 MiB step a 200-char relay is missed about 31% of the time (before, always).
- What you do: nothing. Relay detection is off by default. The attachments count toward the
  plan's staging bound and the global sketch budget; a step that finds no room is counted
  (`bound="plan_step_fps_skipped"`) and its holder's refusal names lost room instead. Plan
  answers over 1 MiB are not covered yet.
