## The per-key rolling budget that nothing enforced is gone

**Startup:** no notice

The cost tracker carried a per-key rolling budget (a hard limit over a day, week or month window, with a warning at 80 %). No config key or endpoint ever set one, and nothing checked it. A key's report therefore always said `hard_limit_usd: null` and `budget_status: "Ok"`, which reads like a limit that holds. The limits that are enforced are the budget enforcer's daily caps: global, per tool and per key.

- What you lose: the library items `BudgetConfig`, `BudgetStatus`, `CostTracker::set_key_budget`, `CostTracker::check_budget`, `KeyCost::budget_status` and the `KeyCost.budget` field.
- What stays the same: `/api/costs`, `/ui/api/costs` and the admin cost report keep every field. `hard_limit_usd` is `null` and `budget_status` is `"Ok"`, as before. Neither judges a limit; the dashboard's Costs page will show the enforced per-key limit from the budget enforcer instead.
- What you should know: the report's `window_24h`, `window_7d` and `window_30d` history is kept in memory and starts again at zero after a restart. The enforced daily totals are saved and survive a restart.
- What you do: delete library calls to the removed items. Nothing to change in configuration.
