## Short credentials warn, and refuse under the hardened posture

**Startup:** no notice, a log warning for a literal or `env:` `auth.bearer_token` under 20 characters; refuses to start, only under `security.posture: hardened` when that token is under 20 characters

What you gain: a short shared credential can be guessed online, and the
gateway now says so. A literal or `env:` `auth.bearer_token` under 20
characters logs one warning at load, naming the field and the floor but never
the value. Under `security.posture: hardened` the same token refuses the load
with an error that names `auth.bearer_token` and the fix (20 or more
characters, or `auto`) and never prints the token. `auto`, `file:` and
`keychain:` tokens are not measured: `auto` and the token `init` stores are
random. The "Invalid token" warning now carries the peer address of the
connection, so repeated failures can be traced to their source.

What you lose: a hardened gateway whose bearer token is under 20 characters no
longer starts until the token is replaced. A config read only to be rewritten
still loads, so the token can be changed with the usual tools.

`mcp-gateway hash-key` refuses a new key under 20 bytes (exit 2). Digests
already in `auth.api_keys` keep working, and `hash-key --verify` still checks a
shorter key against its digest.
