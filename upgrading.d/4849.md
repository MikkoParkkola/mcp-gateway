## Activity: an untraceable integrity failure no longer reads as no_record

**Startup:** no notice, nothing changes at startup

**Who is affected.** Clients of the Activity list (`/ui/api/activity`), the receipt and the signed export that read `effect` or `answer_integrity` per call, or the list response and export manifest as a whole.

**Before.** A call's delivery record is joined to it by the record's trace id. A tamperer who edited a delivery record could also remove its trace id, so the failure attached to no trace and the original call read `effect: no_record`, an answer that looks benign.

**After.** A line in the invocation log that fails its integrity check (invalid hash or signature, or a missing signature after signing began) and carries no usable trace id (missing, not a string, or empty) is remembered by the read. Every call older than that line that would have read `no_record` reads `effect: unknown` with `answer_integrity: "indeterminate"` instead, and is counted in `answer_integrity_failed`. The list response and the export manifest gain `answer_integrity_unattributed`: `{"log": "invocation", "segment": <n>, "offset": <n or null>}` for the first such line seen, or `null`. The receipt gives the same answer. A plain unsigned line written before signing began does not count, and a call written after the failing line is not affected. A call with no trace id is covered too.

**What you do.** Nothing on the gateway. A client that renders `answer_integrity` shows `indeterminate` as an unresolved answer: the log holds a line that failed its check and cannot be tied to a call, so this call's delivery cannot be ruled out. The values are now `duplicate`, `indeterminate`, `invalid`, `unsigned` (and `unverifiable` on a found record).
