## A refused call releases its key; a call that may have run keeps it

**Startup:** no notice

**What you lose.** A same-key retry now gets the may-have-run notice, instead of a fresh run, after:

- a bridged exchange (a backend that asked the client a question) that failed, ran out of budget, was refused or was dropped after its first round reached the backend;
- a legacy call dropped while the gateway was asking the client;
- an HTTP resend after an expired session that could not connect.

To run the call again, send it under a new idempotency key.

**What you gain.** A call the gateway refused inside the backend's own send, before anything was sent (for example a secret rule that cannot be applied), is recorded as refused, and its key is released, so a same-key retry after the cause is fixed runs instead of being answered from the stored failure.

**What you do.** Nothing.
