## OpenAPI imports refuse specs over 32 MiB

**Startup:** no notice

**What you gain.** A spec URL can no longer make the gateway read an unbounded body: the download stops at 32 MiB, before anything is parsed, and the import answers 502 naming the budget.

**What you lose.** A spec over 32 MiB cannot be imported from its URL, in the web UI or through `POST /ui/api/import/openapi` and its preview.

**What you do.** Nothing, for a spec under 32 MiB. For a larger one, split it and import each part. Pasting it into the import's `spec` field does not help by default: request bodies stop at `server.max_body_size`, 10 MiB unless raised.
