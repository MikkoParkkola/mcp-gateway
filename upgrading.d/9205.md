## Allowed calls name the rule that allowed them

**Startup:** no notice

**Who is affected.** Anyone who reads the decision object in invocation records (the transparency log, SIEM export, or a dashboard built on them) and treats every allowed call as `default_allow`.

**Before.** Every allowed call recorded `kind: default_allow` with no `rule_id`, whichever check let it through.

**After.** An allowed call records the most specific rule that allowed it, with its keyed `rule_id`: a matched identity grant (`kind: grant`, plus the configured `grant_id`), then an mTLS rule (`mtls`), then a tool-policy allow-list entry (`tool_policy`), then a firewall rule that let a finding through (`firewall`). A firewall rule that softens a blocking finding to allow outranks a grant. A call no rule allowed, or one allowed only by a policy's default action, still records `default_allow`. Refused calls are unchanged. A call answered from a cached result still records `not_redecided`.

**What to do.** Nothing in the gateway's configuration. A reader that filters allowed calls on `kind == default_allow` should filter on the outcome instead.
