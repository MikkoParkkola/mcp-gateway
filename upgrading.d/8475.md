## A capability account's error-rate cooldown is checked after the credential step

**Startup:** no notice

What you gain: a caller whose grant on a capability's personal account was
revoked can no longer learn that the capability tool exists because other
callers pushed it into error-rate cooldown. A stored task read answers it as
before the cooldown (absent), and a live call gets the account's own answer.

What changes for a caller who can see the tool, on an account-bearing
capability or a per-user backend:

- The cooldown refusal comes after the credential step, and a keyed replay of
  an earlier finished call to a tool in cooldown is refused with the same
  error -32000 (HTTP 200 on `/mcp`) instead of being served from the stored
  result. The same key is served again once the cooldown ends.
- A signed call refused for the cooldown keeps its nonce spent. Retry with a
  fresh nonce.
- A stored task read of the tool answers retryable while the cooldown lasts,
  and serves once it ends. It costs one credential-store read.

What stays the same: a capability on an explicit `shared` account, and any
shared backend, still checks the cooldown in the invocation policy.
