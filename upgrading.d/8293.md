## One caller cannot fill the continuation pool

**Startup:** no notice, a caller asking a 65th open question is refused while running

What you gain: the table of open questions and confirmations (4,096 per
replica) is shared, and one caller could hold all of it for up to five
minutes, refusing every other caller's questions and confirmations. Each
caller now holds at most 64.

What changes: a caller that already has 64 rounds open is refused a new one
with the same error a full pool gives. A caller is its credential, verified
identity, trusted subject or stdio process: the same caller whether it calls
directly, through a task, or on any backend. Nothing already open is evicted;
an answered round frees its slot at once, and an abandoned one when it
expires, up to five minutes later.

What to do: nothing for ordinary use. A client that keeps more than 64
questions open at once should answer some before asking more.
