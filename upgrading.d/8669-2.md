## Three severity enum paths removed

**Startup:** no notice

**What you gain.** One canonical severity type,
`mcp_gateway::security::Severity`, instead of three identical paths to it. The
variants (`Low`, `Medium`, `High`, `Critical`) and their serialized strings
are the same under the canonical path.

**What you lose.** The three re-export paths no longer resolve: the firewall,
response-inspection and context-integrity severity aliases are gone, so any
`use` of them fails to compile.

**What you do.** Replace each old path with
`mcp_gateway::security::Severity`:

- `mcp_gateway::security::firewall::Severity` -> `mcp_gateway::security::Severity`
- `mcp_gateway::security::response_inspect::Severity` -> `mcp_gateway::security::Severity`
- `mcp_gateway::context_integrity::ContextIntegritySeverity` -> `mcp_gateway::security::Severity`
