## A health-check rebuild keeps the working connection until its replacement starts

**Startup:** no notice

**What you lose.** Health checks no longer run on a fixed clock grid. A pass slower than
`failsafe.health_check.interval` (a failing rebuild can take two discovery timeouts) is followed
by a full interval before the next one, where earlier builds started the next pass at once.

**What you gain.** When a health check rebuilds an HTTP backend that cannot start again without
a login (its OAuth token lapsed, or its token refresh stalled until the check timed out), the
working connection stays in service instead of being taken out and left empty. A connection
still in use when its replacement starts is closed only after its last call finishes. A backend
upgraded to the 2026 protocol revision is detected by the rebuild's probe. A failing rebuild is
retried at most once per interval.

**What you do.** Nothing.
