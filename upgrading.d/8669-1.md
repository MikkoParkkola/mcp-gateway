## Firewall severity gains Critical

**Startup:** no notice

**What you gain.** The firewall severity is now the shared
`mcp_gateway::security::Severity`, with `Critical` alongside `High`, `Medium`
and `Low`. `Critical` blocks like `High`, and the type orders
(`Low < Medium < High < Critical`) and deserializes from the same snake-case
strings it serializes to.

**What you lose.** The implicit discriminants change: the variants are now
declared Low, Medium, High, Critical, so any index-based encoding of the old
three-variant order changes: `High` moves from 0 to 2, `Low` from 2 to 0, `Medium`
stays 1, and `Critical` is 3. The serialized strings and the firewall audit rows
are unchanged, and no firewall producer emits `critical` today.

**What you do.** Handle `Critical` in every exhaustive match on the firewall
severity, treating it as a block like `High`, and do not rely on the enum's
numeric value. A Critical anomaly-only block answers -32002, like High.
