## Gateway-only answer members are removed from backend answers

**Startup:** no notice

What you gain: these members are the gateway's word about an answer: its
findings, its contract and integrity verdicts, its cost notes and its
signature. A client or a model reads them as the gateway's. A backend could
send any of them itself, and when the gateway's own check had nothing to say,
the backend's copy was delivered as the gateway's verdict. Now only the
gateway writes them.

What changes:

- On every route (`/mcp`, `/mcp/{name}`, stdio, task results and replays), a
  backend's copy of each member above is removed when its answer arrives,
  from the result, a task's delivered result and an error's `data`.
- One warning per answer names the backend, the method and the removed
  members, never their values.
- A backend's `trace_id`, `recovery` and `predicted_next` are delivered as
  before.
- A task result stored before this change is not stripped when it is
  served. A row from a published 4.0 pre-release is refused anyway, because
  it records no call bindings. The one exposure is a row written at record
  version 9 by an unreleased development build, kept indefinitely by
  `tasks.default_ttl_ms: 0`. If you ran a development build, clear the task
  store once after upgrading.
