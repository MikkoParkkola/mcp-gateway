## Firewall rules and the tool policy apply on reload

**Startup:** no notice

**What changes.** Editing a rule under `security.firewall.rules`, turning
`security.firewall.credential_redaction` on or off, or changing `security.tool_policy` and
reloading the config changes the next call: a new `block` rule refuses it, a lifted deny lets it
through. Before 4.0.0 these waited for a restart, and the reload reported `security` as needing
one. A request already in flight keeps the rules it arrived under for its own checks. Its answer is
judged under those rules and the ones in force when the answer is scanned, and the stricter result
stands: a reload cannot loosen an answer already in flight, and a tightening reload reaches it.
A task result as it settles, a watch poll or an event has no request behind it and is judged by
the rules in force when it is scanned.

**Still restart-only.** The firewall's other settings (`enabled`, the detectors and their
thresholds, collusion detection, the audit log) still apply only at a restart and are still
reported.
